The Complete Trombone Duets

European Gramophone / ALOADED · since 2024

A recording of the complete concert duets led by Christian Lindberg on his own label: Vol. I (released February 11, 2025) features Lindberg, Joseph Alessi, Ian Bousfield, Peter Moore and other pairings; the series continues with singles (Duet No. 19 in 2025 and 2026 with different partners). The Lindberg–Alessi–Bousfield trio also plays on stage: a live recording appeared on “Three Tenors at Valudden” (2025).

Kelton Koch & Adam Hanna

Three concert duets · recorded 2022

Two University of Rhode Island faculty members recorded Duets No. 2, No. 8, and No. 38, with Christian Lindberg taking part in the project. The release date differs across sources (2022 on Discogs, autumn 2023 in the announcement), so no year is given here.

Daniel Ridder, tuba

Neue Etüden für Bass- und Kontrabasstuba · EP, 2026

The tuba branch of the legacy: six etudes in a new edition with piano, about 17 minutes. Abroad the “70 Studies” also live as tuba repertoire — from Robert King’s school edition in the US to requirements in Graz and Shanghai (see The school and its lineage, “Blazhevich in the world’s requirements today”).
